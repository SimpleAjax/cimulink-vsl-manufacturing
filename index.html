Repetitive & packaging manufacturing

From Excel sheets to a factory that runs on one system.

Representative packaging manufacturer scenario

Production cannot see what stores has. Sales cannot see what production can commit. Management receives a version of the numbers only after the moment to act has passed.

This reference implementation connects sales, production, inventory and accounts around the shop-floor workflow - so the system reflects the work instead of becoming another spreadsheet to maintain.

What the operating view is designed to change:
  • Reports that once needed manual compilation update from live transactions
  • Stock position is visible before customer commitments are made
  • Leaders can review the business from exceptions instead of chasing updates

Make-to-order industrial manufacturing

From “let me check” to an answer the team can stand behind.

Representative industrial assembly scenario

For pumps, skids and custom assemblies, a delivery date depends on material, supplier promises, work orders, testing and dispatch. Without one connected picture, the answer is a chain of calls.

This reference implementation makes the dependency visible: what is missing, which supplier delay matters, what work is waiting, and which customer commitment needs attention.

What the operating view is designed to change:
  • Customer commitments are linked to material and production dependencies
  • Shortages are seen in terms of the order they affect
  • Teams manage exceptions and actions, not a long list of reports

Jewellery manufacturing

High-value inventory without “approximately”.

Representative jewellery manufacturing scenario

Gold, stones and semi-finished goods move through stores, artisans, production and job work. When material is valuable, an unclear register or late wastage calculation is not a small administrative issue.

This reference implementation follows material by weight, stage and linked customer order - connecting issue, receipt, expected return, wastage and approval records.

What the operating view is designed to change:
  • Material issued to job work remains traceable until return
  • Wastage can be investigated by production order and stage
  • Purity-wise material position is available without waiting for a physical count
