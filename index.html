Repetitive & packaging manufacturing

Know tomorrow's production plan before the line stops.

Representative packaging manufacturer scenario

Production does not know what stores has. Sales does not know what production can promise. By the time the owner sees the numbers, the shift or delivery is already affected.

The reference setup puts sales, stores, production and accounts on the same page. The team can see what is short, what is running late and what to do next.

What changes day to day:
  • Reports that once needed manual compilation update from live transactions
  • Stock position is visible before customer commitments are made
  • Leaders can review the business from exceptions instead of chasing updates

Make-to-order industrial manufacturing

Give customers a delivery date without five phone calls.

Representative industrial assembly scenario

For pumps, skids and custom assemblies, a delivery date depends on material, supplier delivery, work orders, testing and dispatch. Without one clear view, the answer is a chain of calls.

The reference setup shows what is missing, which supplier delay matters, what work is waiting and which customer order needs action now.

What changes day to day:
  • Customer commitments are linked to material and production dependencies
  • Shortages are seen in terms of the order they affect
  • Teams manage exceptions and actions, not a long list of reports

Jewellery manufacturing

Know where every gram is before it becomes a stock question.

Representative jewellery manufacturing scenario

Gold, stones and semi-finished goods move through stores, artisans, production and job work. When material is valuable, “approximately” is not good enough.

The reference setup follows material by weight, stage and customer order. It connects issue, return, wastage and approvals in one place.

What changes day to day:
  • Material issued to job work remains traceable until return
  • Wastage can be investigated by production order and stage
  • Purity-wise material position is available without waiting for a physical count
